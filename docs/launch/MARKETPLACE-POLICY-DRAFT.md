# Marketplace cancellation and review policy draft

## Before the final estimate is accepted

Either party may cancel the selected bid through YardPilotUSA. YardPilotUSA records
who cancelled, when, and why. The request and accepted bid are retained as an
audit record rather than deleted.

## After the client accepts the final estimate

Cancellation becomes a request that the other party must approve or decline.
The signed estimate, disclosed cancellation terms, applicable law, materials
already purchased, work already performed, and payment/refund rules control.
YardPilotUSA preserves the request, messages, estimate, signature and payment
history and does not automatically decide legal fault.

## Reviews

Only the client connected to a paid or completed YardPilotUSA marketplace project
may submit one review. Reviews are labeled as verified YardPilotUSA projects.
Reviews may be moderated for threats, hate, personal information, spam, fraud or
other abuse. Reviews must not be rejected merely because they are negative.
Businesses may publish one response after a review is published.

This draft requires attorney review before broad commercial release.
