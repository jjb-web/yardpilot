# Legal, Ownership, and Company Tasks

These tasks are not solved by source code. Complete them with qualified Oregon
business, legal, tax, and payroll professionals before broad commercial launch.

- Form the legal entity and obtain the EIN directly through official channels.
- Open the company bank account.
- Put GitHub, domain, Google, Stripe, Supabase, Vercel, and email under
  company-controlled ownership with documented recovery access and 2FA.
- Sign a founder/IP agreement covering ownership, vesting, pre-existing code,
  assignment, duties, decisions, expenses, departure, confidentiality, sale, and
  dissolution.
- Confirm every contributor has assigned necessary IP rights.
- Review the included legal pages; they are beta scaffolds, not final legal advice.
- Create retention, dispute, refund, security-incident, and law-enforcement
  response procedures.
- Use a real payroll provider for wages, withholding, W-2s, and payroll filings.

Do not rely on a source-code watermark as a substitute for signed agreements.
