# Deferred Roadmap

Do not add these before the launch-hardening test gate passes:

- Real employee payroll or automated wage transfers
- Tax-form generation or EIN collection
- QuickBooks integration; begin with reviewed CSV exports later
- Receipt manager
- AI estimate generation or AI review/moderation decisions
- Google Earth/maps measurement and route/fuel automation
- Integrated messaging
- Browser push notifications
- Cross-workspace transfer of contacts/jobs/estimates/invoices
- Director dashboard spanning unrelated companies
- Full private cost/profit accounting
- Public landscaper reviews without verified-job moderation and appeals

Employee payments remain an external-payment ledger. Reports remain a Beta
operational summary, not accounting, tax, payroll, or audited profit reporting.
